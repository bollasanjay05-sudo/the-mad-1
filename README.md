# THE MAD ENGINEER & CO. — GitHub product gallery update

**What changed:** Real uploaded product photos replace illustration placeholders across the hero, apparel, frames, magnets, drinkware and calendars. Existing category sliders, customization and WhatsApp cart are kept.

## Run locally

`python -m http.server 8000` and open `http://localhost:8000`.

Set `WHATSAPP_NUMBER` in `script.js` to your own business number with country code. Until then, checkout opens WhatsApp's generic share flow, not a direct chat with your business.

## Rights and publishing

The images were supplied by the site owner as visual references and are not represented as the company's own photographs. Some depict recognisable third-party designs, characters or brands. Confirm appropriate rights or replace them with licensed/original commercial images before using publicly as an offer for sale.

The extra category mockups point to assets in the account's existing `themadengineer-co` repository; move these into this repository before making this site fully standalone. No price or availability claims are made.
